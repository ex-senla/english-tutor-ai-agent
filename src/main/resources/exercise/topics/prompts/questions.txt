Grammar focus: Questions (broad grammar section).
Active subtopics: Yes / No Questions, Wh-Questions.

Guidance for exercise generation:
- Treat "Questions" as a broad topic; the exercise may target any of its active subtopics listed above.
- Keep sentences natural and appropriate for the student's CEFR level.
- In fill-in-the-blank, the blank must require applying the selected grammar area; the answer must not be guessable from context alone.
- In multiple choice, the three distractors must be plausible grammar errors typical for learners of this topic.
- If the target vocabulary is provided, use it only as context; the target words are not the answer.
