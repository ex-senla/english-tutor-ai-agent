Grammar focus: Wh-Questions.

Guidance for exercise generation:
- Focus strictly on "Wh-Questions" and its typical forms and usage.
- Keep sentences natural and appropriate for the student's CEFR level.
- In fill-in-the-blank, the blank must require applying "Wh-Questions"; the answer must not be guessable from context alone.
- In multiple choice, the three distractors must be plausible grammar errors typical for learners of "Wh-Questions".
- If the target vocabulary is provided, use it only as context; the target words are not the answer.
